How to talk to AI

Enough Context is your aim

Don't just yell "fetch." Tell the pup something vague and it won't stop and ask — it'll guess the rest and trot back with whatever it assumed you meant. Every detail you leave out is a decision the AI quietly makes for you. The more you describe the throw, the closer it lands.

1
The vague throw

You just say "build it."

So the pup guesses the rest.

What you said →

"Build me a weather app I can run locally in my laptop browser."

What it quietly assumes

  • Grabs one random weather source — whichever it reaches first
  • Picks a location for you, or whatever your IP says
  • Today's temperature, a few generic cards, stock styling
  • No moon, no sunrise, no way to change the city
✗ A weather app — just not yours.
The pup

Same pup. Your aim decides what it brings back.

2
The aimed throw

You say what & why.

So it brings back what you pictured.

What you said →

"I track weather from three sites every morning and average them — different models, different forecasts. Build one app that combines all three into a single, more reliable forecast. Default to Dublin, but let me change location. Add moon phase, sunrise & sunset, and make the website look modern and sleek."

What it brings back

  • Pulls all three sources, blends one averaged forecast
  • Defaults to Dublin — and the location is changeable
  • Moon phase, sunrise & sunset built in
  • Designed around your real goal: a forecast you trust
✓ The app you actually pictured.
01

Say the why.

"I want a forecast I can trust" steers it more than any list of features ever will.

02

Name the musts.

The defaults, the location, the exact sources. Anything you don't pin down, it picks for you.

03

Add the extras.

Moon, sunrise, sunset — the little details only you know you wanted. Unsaid means unbuilt.
