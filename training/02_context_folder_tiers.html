Pack the pup's bag like a budget.

Every toy the pup carries adds weight to every walk. There are four places to put things — on the collar (always worn), in the bag (today's walk), in the toy bin (left at home), and off to the shop (the costly one to avoid).

01

Stickers, not stories.

One short label per toy. The pup just needs to know what's inside — not the whole story.

02

Throw out chewed toys.

When something's done, put it away. Don't leave a "this used to be here" note — it still adds weight.

03

One toy, one home.

Don't keep two copies of the same toy in different bins. Pick one home and point everything else at it.

04

Fetch when asked.

Don't tell the pup "grab everything from the bin." Say "the bin is over there — fetch only when I ask."
